## Full Description

For Description / About / Product Details fields.

```text
【Overview】
MP3 to Text (mp3totext.io) is an online AI speech-to-text tool that helps users convert MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, and other audio/video files into editable, searchable text. No software installation is required—everything runs in the browser.

【Core Features】
· Free online transcription: Try instantly as a guest; create a free account to save history and unlock a higher daily allowance
· File upload: Upload local MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, and other audio or video files to start transcribing
· Broad format support: Works with common formats including MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, MP4, MOV, and WebM
· 150+ languages: Auto-detect or manually select the spoken language
· Timestamps and speaker labels: Produce structured transcripts that are easy to search, quote, and review
· AI enhancements: Generate summaries and mind maps to understand long recordings faster
· In-browser editing: Edit segments and use search-and-replace across the full transcript
· Flexible export: Export to TXT, PDF, DOCX, CSV, SRT, VTT, and more for subtitles, documents, and research notes
· Subtitle generation: Create SRT/VTT subtitle files for video production workflows

【Who It Is For】
· Podcasters, video creators, and social media teams
· Journalists, editors, and media production teams
· Students, teachers, and researchers
· Professionals who need meeting notes, interview transcripts, and business documentation

【Use Cases】
· Turn podcasts, interviews, and lecture recordings into written transcripts
· Generate subtitles and content assets for video production
· Organize meetings, talks, and interviews more efficiently
· Extract key points from long audio with summaries and mind maps
· Repurpose audio into articles, reports, newsletters, and research notes

【Free and Paid Plans】
· Guest users: Up to 2 transcriptions per day, up to 30 minutes per file
· Free accounts: Up to 3 transcriptions per day, up to 50 minutes per file—no credit card required
· Paid plans: Billed by transcription minutes with Monthly / Yearly subscriptions and one-time minute packs (800 / 2000 minutes); paid users can process files up to 10 hours / 5 GB each

【Why MP3 to Text】
Everything runs in your browser—no download required. Upload audio, get a transcript with timestamps and speaker labels, edit online, and export to TXT, SRT, PDF, and more. Free to try as a guest; on clear recordings, accuracy can reach up to 98%.
```
